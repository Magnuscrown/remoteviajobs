About Remote Via Jobs

What We Do

Remote Via Jobs is a free remote job platform that connects global talent with remote opportunities from around the world. We aggregate remote job listings from trusted platforms and help job seekers find, evaluate, and apply for remote roles — all in one place.

Our Mission

Our mission is simple: make remote work accessible to everyone, everywhere. Whether you're in South Africa, Ghana, Nigeria, USA, UK, or India, we believe you deserve access to the same opportunities as anyone else.

AI-Powered Job Matching

Our platform includes an AI Match Checker that helps you see how well your CV matches a specific job. Simply upload your CV, click "Check My Match," and get instant feedback on your match score, missing ATS keywords, and personalized recommendations.

We also have an RVJ AI assistant available 24/7 to answer your questions about remote work, interviews, and career growth.

Privacy First

We take your privacy seriously. Unlike most job platforms:

  • No account required — use the site freely, no signup
  • Your CV is never saved — it's analyzed and deleted instantly
  • No tracking or data selling — we don't collect personal data
  • 100% free forever — no hidden fees, no premium tiers

Who We Are

Remote Via Jobs was founded by Magnus Crown, a young entrepreneur passionate about making remote work accessible to people worldwide. Built with modern technology and AI, we're committed to helping millions of job seekers find their dream remote jobs.
