Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

🔒 Summary: We never save your CV. No account needed. We only store your email if YOU choose to subscribe to job alerts. Save Job uses your own browser, not our servers. We never sell your data. Ever.

1. Our Privacy Commitment

Remote Via Jobs is built on a privacy-first philosophy. We believe job seekers should be able to use a job platform without sacrificing their personal data. This policy explains exactly what we do — and don't — collect.

2. What We DO NOT Collect

We do not collect, store, or share:

  • Your CV, resume, or any documents you upload (analyzed in real-time and deleted instantly)
  • Your name, phone number, or home address
  • Your IP address or location data
  • Your browsing history or search queries
  • Any account information (we don't have accounts)
  • Your chat history with RVJ AI (deleted after 10 minutes)

3. What We DO Collect (Only When You Choose)

We only collect information when you voluntarily provide it:

📧 Job Alerts (Email Subscription): If you choose to subscribe to job alerts, we store your email address and job preference in our secure database. This is used ONLY to send you job alerts matching your interest. You can unsubscribe at any time by clicking the unsubscribe link in any email.

❤️ Saved Jobs: When you click "Save Job," the job is saved only in your own browser (using a technology called localStorage). We never see, access, or store this data on our servers. It disappears if you clear your browser data or switch devices.

4. How the AI Match Checker Works

When you upload your CV to our AI Match Checker:

  • Your CV is sent securely to our trusted AI processing partners
  • The AI analyzes it against the job description
  • You receive your match score and keyword suggestions
  • Your CV is immediately deleted from memory — it is never stored on our servers or our partners' servers

5. RVJ AI Chat Assistant

Our RVJ AI assistant does not save your conversation history on our servers. When you close the chat, all messages are cleared from your browser after 10 minutes. We do not log, store, or review your chat messages.

6. Cookies and Tracking

We do not use tracking cookies, analytics services, or third-party trackers. Your browser may store small preferences locally (like saved jobs), but we never access them. We do not use Google Analytics or similar tools.

7. Third-Party Links

Our site links to external job platforms and company websites. When you click "Apply Now," you are redirected to the original job source. We are not responsible for their privacy practices. We recommend reviewing their privacy policies before submitting any personal information.

8. Children's Privacy

Remote Via Jobs is not intended for children under 16. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. The latest version will always be available on this page. Continued use of the site means you accept any updates.

10. Contact

If you have privacy questions, contact our founder, Magnus Crown. Contact details are available in the Contact Us section at the bottom of our homepage. You can also ask RVJ AI for help.
