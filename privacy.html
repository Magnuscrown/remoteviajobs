Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

🔒 Summary: We don't save your CV. We don't require accounts. We don't track you. We don't sell your data. Ever.

1. Our Privacy Commitment

Remote Via Jobs is built on a privacy-first philosophy. We believe job seekers should be able to use a job platform without sacrificing their personal data.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect, store, or share:

  • Your name, email, phone number, or address
  • Your CV, resume, or any documents you upload
  • Your IP address or location data
  • Your browsing history or search queries
  • Any account information (we don't have accounts)

3. How the AI Match Checker Works

When you upload your CV to our AI Match Checker:

  • Your CV is sent securely to our trusted AI processing partners
  • The AI analyzes it against the job description
  • You receive your match score and keyword suggestions
  • Your CV is immediately deleted from memory — it is never stored on our servers or our partners' servers

4. AI Chat Assistant

Our RVJ AI assistant does not save your conversation history. When you close the chat, all messages are cleared after 10 minutes. We do not log, store, or review your chat messages.

5. Cookies and Tracking

We do not use tracking cookies, analytics services, or third-party trackers. Your browser may store small preferences locally, but we never access them.

6. Third-Party Links

Our site links to external job platforms and company websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices. We recommend reviewing their privacy policies before submitting any personal information.

7. Children's Privacy

Remote Via Jobs is not intended for children under 16. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. The latest version will always be available on this page.

9. Contact

If you have privacy questions, contact our founder, Magnus Crown. Contact details are available in the Contact Us section at the bottom of our homepage.
