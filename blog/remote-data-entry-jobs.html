Remote Data Entry Jobs: Complete Guide

By Magnus Crown • 6 min read • September 2026

Remote data entry is one of the easiest entry-level remote jobs. No degree, no experience, no special skills required. Here's everything you need to know.

What is Remote Data Entry?

You enter information from one source into a digital system. Examples:

  • Typing scanned documents into Excel
  • Copying info from invoices into accounting software
  • Adding customer info to a CRM
  • Transcribing handwritten notes into text files
  • Updating product listings on e-commerce sites

Salary Range (2026)

  • Entry level: $20,000 - $35,000/year
  • Mid level: $30,000 - $45,000/year
  • Senior / Specialist: $40,000 - $60,000/year
  • Freelance rates: $5 - $15/hour

Data entry pays less than other remote jobs — but it's the fastest way to start earning remotely.

Skills You Need

  • Typing speed: 40+ WPM minimum, 60+ preferred
  • Accuracy: You must be detail-oriented
  • Excel / Google Sheets: Basic formulas and formatting
  • Attention to detail: Mistakes cost companies money
  • Time management: Meet deadlines

That's it. No degree, no certificates needed.

Tools You Should Learn

  • Excel / Google Sheets — Most common tools
  • Microsoft Word — Document formatting
  • Notion / Airtable — Modern databases
  • CRM tools: HubSpot, Salesforce
  • OCR tools: Adobe Acrobat, Google Docs

Free training on YouTube and LinkedIn Learning.

Types of Data Entry Jobs

  • Copy-paste data entry: Easiest, lowest pay
  • Document digitization: Scanning and typing
  • Product listings: Adding items to e-commerce
  • CRM management: Updating customer data
  • Transcription: Audio to text
  • Bookkeeping entry: Financial data into software

How to Get Hired (Step by Step)

  1. Test your typing speed at 10fastfingers.com — aim for 50+ WPM
  2. Learn Excel basics — free on YouTube (2 hours of content)
  3. Create an Upwork profile — start with $5/hour rates
  4. Apply to 20+ jobs per week on multiple platforms
  5. Build reviews — after 5 jobs, raise your rate to $10/hour
  6. Specialize — bookkeeping, product listings, or CRM entry

⚠️ Scam Warning: Real data entry jobs do NOT require you to pay for training, software, or "registration." If they ask for money, it's a scam.

Common Scams

  • "Pay $50 for training materials" — scam
  • "Buy our software to start working" — scam
  • "Send us your bank details to get hired" — scam
  • Anything that pays unrealistically high ($50/hour for data entry) — scam

How to Stand Out

  • Complete a free Excel certification (Google, Coursera)
  • Add typing speed to your CV ("60 WPM, 99% accuracy")
  • Show samples of your work (redacted spreadsheets)
  • Be responsive and professional in communication
  • Deliver early on every job — build reputation

Career Growth Path

  1. Data Entry Clerk (entry)
  2. Data Entry Specialist (6-12 months)
  3. Data Analyst (learn SQL and Excel)
  4. Business Analyst (learn more tools)
  5. Data Scientist (advanced path, requires more training)

Best Industries for Data Entry

  • Healthcare — Medical records, patient data
  • E-commerce — Product listings, order management
  • Finance — Bookkeeping, invoice entry
  • Real estate — Property listings, CRM updates
  • Legal — Document digitization
