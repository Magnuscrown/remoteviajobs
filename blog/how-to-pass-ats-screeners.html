How to Pass ATS Screeners in 2026

By Magnus Crown • 5 min read • September 2026

Here's a hard truth: 75% of CVs are rejected by AI before a human ever sees them. These AI systems are called ATS — Applicant Tracking Systems. If your CV doesn't pass them, you'll never get an interview.

But once you understand how ATS works, you can beat it. Here's how.

What is an ATS?

An ATS is software that companies use to filter CVs. When you apply for a job, your CV goes into the ATS. The system scans your CV for specific keywords that match the job description. If your CV doesn't have enough of those keywords, it gets rejected automatically.

Popular ATS systems: Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, Taleo, and BambooHR.

Rule #1: Mirror the Job Description

The ATS looks for exact keyword matches. If the job says "customer support" and your CV says "client assistance," you fail.

The fix: Copy the job description. Highlight the top 15 keywords. Use them naturally in your CV.

Rule #2: Use Standard Section Headings

ATS systems recognize specific headings. Use exactly these:

  • Professional Summary (or Summary)
  • Work Experience (or Experience)
  • Education
  • Skills
  • Certifications

Don't use creative headings like "My Journey" or "What I Bring."

Rule #3: Avoid Fancy Formatting

ATS cannot read:

  • Tables and columns
  • Images, icons, and logos
  • Text boxes
  • Headers and footers
  • Unusual fonts

Stick to a simple, single-column layout with standard fonts like Arial, Calibri, or Inter.

Rule #4: Save as PDF

PDFs preserve formatting better than Word files. Save your CV as a PDF with a clean filename: FirstName_LastName_CV.pdf.

Rule #5: Include a Skills Section

This is where you stuff keywords. List 10-15 relevant skills as bullet points. Include tools (Zendesk, Slack, Excel), software (Photoshop, Figma), and soft skills (communication, teamwork).

Rule #6: Match Job Titles

If the job is "Customer Success Manager," your CV should say "Customer Success Manager" — not "Client Relations Specialist."

Rule #7: Use Numbers in Experience

ATS scans for metrics. Add numbers to every bullet point:

  • ❌ "Handled customer inquiries"
  • ✅ "Resolved 50+ customer inquiries daily with 95% satisfaction"

Rule #8: No Typos

One typo can get you rejected. Use Grammarly (free) to check your CV before submitting.
