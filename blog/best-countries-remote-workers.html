Best Countries for Remote Workers in 2026

By Magnus Crown • 6 min read • September 2026

Where you live as a remote worker matters. Some countries have better internet, lower taxes, easier visas, and a stronger remote work culture. Here are the top 10 countries for remote workers in 2026.

1. 🇵🇹 Portugal

Portugal's Digital Nomad Visa made it the #1 destination for remote workers. Lisbon and Porto have fast internet, low cost of living (compared to Western Europe), and a huge expat community. Taxes are reasonable, and the weather is excellent.

Best for: Digital nomads wanting European lifestyle on a budget.

2. 🇪🇪 Estonia

Estonia pioneered the Digital Nomad Visa. It's one of the most digital-friendly countries in the world — you can do almost everything online. Tallinn is a tech hub with a great startup scene.

Best for: Tech workers and entrepreneurs.

3. 🇬🇪 Georgia

Georgia offers a 1-year visa-free stay for most nationalities, and foreign income is tax-free for up to 183 days. Tbilisi is affordable, safe, and modern. The internet is fast.

Best for: Remote workers wanting to save money on taxes.

4. 🇹🇭 Thailand

Thailand has long been a favorite for remote workers. Chiang Mai and Bangkok offer cheap living, amazing food, and fast internet. The new Long-Term Resident Visa makes it easier to stay.

Best for: Budget-conscious remote workers.

5. 🇲🇽 Mexico

Mexico City, Playa del Carmen, and Oaxaca are remote worker hotspots. The timezone overlaps with US hours, making it ideal for US-based companies. Cost of living is reasonable.

Best for: US-based remote workers.

6. 🇬🇭 Ghana

Ghana has become a growing hub for African remote workers. Accra has decent internet, English is the official language, and the timezone works with Europe. The cost of living is low.

Best for: African remote workers who want to stay on the continent.

7. 🇰🇪 Kenya

Nairobi's "Silicon Savannah" is a major tech hub. Fast internet, English-speaking, and a growing remote work community. Great for African remote workers.

Best for: African tech workers and customer service professionals.

8. 🇪🇸 Spain

Spain launched its Digital Nomad Visa in 2023. Barcelona, Madrid, and Valencia are popular. Warm weather, great food, and a strong remote work culture.

Best for: European remote workers wanting culture and lifestyle.

9. 🇮🇩 Indonesia (Bali)

Bali's Ubud and Canggu have become remote worker meccas. Cheap living, beautiful scenery, and a huge community. The new "Digital Nomad Visa" (E33G) makes long stays easier.

Best for: Creatives and wellness-focused workers.

10. 🇻🇳 Vietnam

Ho Chi Minh City and Da Nang are amazing for remote workers. Cost of living is one of the lowest in Asia, food is incredible, and the internet is reliable.

Best for: Budget travelers and digital nomads.

What to Consider Before Moving

  • Visa requirements: Can you get a digital nomad visa? How long can you stay?
  • Taxes: Some countries tax foreign income, some don't. Research carefully.
  • Internet speed: Check local speeds before committing
  • Timezones: Will you be able to work with your clients' hours?
  • Cost of living: Rent, food, transport, health insurance
  • Community: Are there other remote workers nearby?
  • Safety: Check travel advisories before moving
  • Banking: Can you receive and spend money easily?

Best Tools for Remote Workers Abroad

  • Banking: Wise, Revolut, Payoneer
  • VPN: NordVPN, Mullvad
  • Wi-Fi finder: WiFi Map, Workfrom
  • Community: Nomad List, Remote Year
  • Insurance: SafetyWing, World Nomads

Don't Move Without a Job

Moving to a cheaper country sounds great — but only if you have income. Land a remote job first, save 3-6 months of expenses, then move.
