Best Remote Job Platforms for Africans in 2026

By Magnus Crown • 6 min read • September 2026

Finding remote work from Africa used to be hard. Not anymore. In 2026, thousands of companies hire remote workers from Ghana, Nigeria, Kenya, South Africa, and across the continent. The key is knowing where to look.

Here are the best remote job platforms for Africans — verified, safe, and actively hiring.

1. Remote Via Jobs (Our Own!)

We aggregate remote jobs from multiple trusted platforms into one place. Best of all: our AI Match Checker helps you see how well your CV fits each job — 100% free, no signup required.

2. Remotive

One of the most trusted remote job platforms. Filter by role, location, and salary. Jobs are curated and updated daily. Many listings accept applicants from Africa.

3. We Work Remotely

The largest remote work community on the internet. Over 100,000 monthly visitors. Jobs include engineering, design, marketing, and customer support.

4. Remote.co

Curated remote jobs with a focus on quality. Many jobs are open globally, including Africa. Great for customer service, marketing, and tech roles.

5. Jobicy

A modern remote job board with a clean interface. Strong on tech, design, and marketing roles. Many listings are open to applicants from any country.

6. Toptal

For elite freelancers and developers. Toptal accepts only the top 3% of applicants — but pays very well. Great for experienced African developers.

7. Andela

Africa-focused talent platform. They hire African engineers and place them with global companies. Excellent for software engineers in Ghana, Nigeria, Kenya, and Uganda.

8. Upwork & Fiverr

Freelance marketplaces where you can start with small gigs and grow. Best for freelancers with skills in writing, design, development, or marketing.

9. LinkedIn

Yes, LinkedIn still works. Set your location to "Remote" and filter by "Worldwide." Many companies now post exclusively remote roles there.

10. Wellfound (formerly AngelList)

Best for startup jobs. Many early-stage startups hire globally. Compensation often includes equity, which can be very valuable.

💡 Pro Tips for African Applicants

  • Use a professional email — not something like "badboy123@..."
  • Mention your timezone — companies love knowing you can overlap with their hours
  • Highlight your English level — many remote jobs are in English
  • Get a payment method — Payoneer, Wise, and Geegpay work well in Africa
  • Don't pay for jobs — legitimate employers never charge you to apply
  • Verify companies — check their website and LinkedIn before sharing personal info

Common Scams to Avoid

Some sites promise "guaranteed remote jobs" for a fee. Those are scams. Legitimate platforms never charge job seekers.
