How to Stay Productive Working From Home

By Magnus Crown • 5 min read • September 2026

Working from home sounds like a dream — until you realize your bed is 3 meters away, Netflix is one click away, and there's no one watching. Productivity becomes 100% your responsibility. Here are 10 proven strategies to stay focused.

1. Create a Dedicated Workspace

Never work from your bed or couch. Your brain needs to associate one specific place with work. Even a small desk in the corner works. The key: when you sit there, you work. When you leave, you're off.

2. Stick to a Schedule

Set fixed working hours. Wake up at the same time, start work at the same time, finish at the same time. Your brain loves routine. If you need to, "clock in" by writing your start time on paper.

3. Dress for Work (At Least a Little)

You don't need a suit. But changing out of pajamas into "work clothes" (even a clean t-shirt) signals to your brain that it's time to focus. Studies show this simple change boosts productivity by 20%.

4. Use the Pomodoro Technique

Work in focused blocks:

  • 25 minutes of deep focus
  • 5-minute break
  • Repeat 4 times, then take a 20-30 minute break

Apps like Pomofocus.io and Forest help you stay on track.

5. Turn Off Notifications

Put your phone in another room. Silence email, Slack, and social media notifications during focus blocks. The average person loses 23 minutes of focus every time they're interrupted.

6. Plan Tomorrow Tonight

Before you finish work for the day, write down your top 3 priorities for tomorrow. When you start the next day, you already know what to do — no wasted time deciding.

7. Take Real Breaks

Don't "break" by scrolling Instagram. That's not a break; that's more screen time. Real breaks:

  • Walk outside for 5 minutes
  • Drink water
  • Stretch
  • Look out the window
  • Make tea or coffee

8. Communicate With Your Team

Remote work can feel isolating. Over-communicate:

  • Send a good morning message
  • Share what you're working on
  • Ask questions early
  • Share wins at the end of the day

9. Protect Your Energy

  • Sleep 7-8 hours
  • Exercise 3-4 times per week
  • Drink 2+ liters of water daily
  • Eat real food, not just snacks
  • Take a full day off each week

10. End Work at a Set Time

Without an office to leave, it's easy to work until midnight. Set a hard stop. Close your laptop. Turn off notifications. Say to yourself, out loud: "Work is done. It's my time now."

Best Tools for Remote Workers

  • Task management: Notion, Todoist, Trello
  • Time tracking: Toggl, Clockify
  • Focus: Forest, Focus To-Do
  • Communication: Slack, Zoom, Loom
  • File storage: Google Drive, Dropbox
  • Notes: Notion, Obsidian, Apple Notes

Common Productivity Killers

  • Working from bed or couch
  • No fixed schedule
  • Checking phone every 5 minutes
  • Eating at your desk
  • Not taking breaks
  • Saying yes to every meeting
  • Perfectionism (done is better than perfect)

The Bottom Line

Productivity isn't about working more hours. It's about working focused hours. 4 hours of deep focus beats 10 hours of distracted work every time.
