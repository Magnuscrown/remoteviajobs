Frequently Asked Questions

Is Remote Via Jobs really free? ▼
Yes, 100% free forever. No account needed, no signup, no hidden fees. We make money through ads and partnerships, not by charging job seekers.
Do I need to create an account? ▼
No. You can browse all jobs, use the AI Match Checker, chat with RVJ AI, and apply to jobs without ever creating an account.
How does the AI Match Checker work? ▼
Click any job → "View Details" → scroll to the AI Match Checker → upload or paste your CV → click "Check My Match." Our AI analyzes your CV against the job and gives you a match score, missing keywords, and personalized tips — in about 10-20 seconds.
Do you save my CV? ▼
Absolutely not. Your CV is sent securely to our AI
